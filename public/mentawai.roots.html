Rooted in Mentawai Heritage

Preserving Ancient Mentawai Culture Through Regenerative Tourism

Mentawai Roots was founded by native Siberut islanders and eco-enthusiasts dedicated to showcasing the authentic Mentawai way of life. We bridge travelers directly with native Uma longhouses and Sikerei shamans—ensuring 100% fair compensation to local indigenous communities while protecting rainforest ecosystems.

Whether paddling up jungle rivers, riding iconic waves like Lance’s Right and Macaronis, or learning traditional wood carving, every expedition is designed for maximum cultural respect and zero-waste impact.
